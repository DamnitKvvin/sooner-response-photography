What I Do

Public Safety Media

Photography and video created for Oklahoma fire departments, EMS agencies, law enforcement, and the public safety community.

On-Scene Coverage

Photography documenting emergency responses, major incidents, and the work taking place on scene.

  • • Working fires and emergency incidents
  • • Multi-agency responses
  • • Day and nighttime coverage

Vehicle Photography

Professional photography of department vehicles for websites, social media, recruiting, public information, and archives.

  • • Fire, EMS, and law enforcement vehicles
  • • Station and roadside setups
  • • Exterior and detail photography

Lighting & Video

Photography and short-form video showcasing emergency lighting, warning equipment, and the vehicles they're mounted on.

  • • Emergency lighting demonstrations
  • • Siren and warning-device videos
  • • Nighttime lighting photography

Interior & Equipment

A closer look at the equipment, technology, and workspaces inside public safety vehicles.

  • • Cab and interior photography
  • • Consoles and equipment layouts
  • • POV and walkthrough video
