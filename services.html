What I Do

The Work

Same idea as a lot of public safety photographers out west — listen to the radio, roll on the good stuff, and shoot the trucks when departments want them looking sharp.

On-Scene

I listen to radio traffic and roll when a good call comes out. Fire, EMS, PD — if I can make it, I'm there shooting.

  • • Structure fires and working incidents
  • • Multi-agency scenes
  • • Night work

Apparatus Shoots

Photo shoots of the rigs. Engines, tankers, medics, cruisers — I haven't really done people shots, just the vehicles.

  • • Station and roadside setups
  • • Clean detail shots of the truck
  • • Whatever the department wants shown off

Lighting & Siren Videos

Lighting showcases and siren demos. The kind of clips departments actually post.

  • • Lightbar and scene lighting
  • • Siren and air horn demos
  • • Night shots of the rig lit up

Equipment & POV

Walkthroughs of the cab, controllers, and gear. Sometimes that's just me in the seat with the Metas messing with the box.

  • • Cab and controller walkthroughs
  • • POV clips from inside the vehicle
  • • Short social-ready videos

Need photos of a truck or want me out on a call? Just say so.
