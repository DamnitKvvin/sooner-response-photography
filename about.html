About

Who I Am

Sooner Response is an Oklahoma-based public safety media project focused on documenting the people, vehicles, equipment, and work of the state's emergency services.

What started with a camera and an interest in public safety has grown into a way of documenting the agencies and communities that keep Oklahoma moving. My work includes fire departments, EMS, law enforcement, and other public safety organizations across the state.

A large part of my work centers around vehicles and equipment — emergency vehicles, lighting, interiors, consoles, and the gear used every day in the field. I also provide on-scene coverage and video when circumstances allow.

I'm based in Oklahoma and travel wherever the work takes me. Whether it's a department vehicle that needs new photos, an event worth documenting, or an incident happening in the community, the goal is simple: document it well and preserve it.
